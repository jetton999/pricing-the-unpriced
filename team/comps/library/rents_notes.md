## 3. NNN vs gross caveats

### Takeaway
Rows mix bases: NNN 33, MG 17, gross/FSG 10, unknown 3 among the commercial rows with a $/SF figure. A gross rent and a NNN rent at the same number are not equivalent, and I found no sourced Baltimore figure for typical NNN expenses to gross them up.

### Cited findings
- "Plus All Utilities" listings were entered as modified gross (R-060, R-062). The exception is R-022: its text says the tenant also pays taxes and insurance, so it was entered as NNN.
- Coworking and FSG office rows include utilities and services (R-003, R-038, R-048, R-039). Their per-SF figures are inflated further by very small suites (R-019, R-048).
- In-place rents are not asking rents:
  - 3217 Greenmount NNN lease through 2033 (R-004).
  - 3409 Greenmount lease from Nov 2025 (R-005, R-008).
  - Both come from for-sale investment listings.
- The only stated expense figures are unclear:
  - The O.C. Haley flyer reads "NNN is $5.19/sf/mo" (R-075). The unit is probably per year.
  - The 2220 N Charles page shows a "$0.50 /SF/Yr" CAM label (R-021).

### Inferences
- When comparing across bases, add a sourced NNN expense estimate to the NNN rows, or deduct one from the gross rows. Do not mix the two bases in one range without that adjustment.
- Most Waverly rows are NNN, so a Waverly rent range should be stated as NNN.

### Gaps
- No sourced Baltimore NNN expense load (taxes, insurance and CAM per SF) for small rowhouse retail.

## 4. Gaps and data-quality flags

### Takeaway
Bike shop has no rows at all, foodmaker is thin, venue has only proxies, and the Waverly tier has few asking rents. Listing dates are mostly not shown, so figure_year is usually the year the listing was seen (2026).

### Cited findings
- **bike**: no listing found anywhere that names bike or repair use. Closest Waverly lead: 609 E 25th St, 2,300 SF, built out as "Quick Service" with a garage. Its rent is "Rate Upon Request", so it is not in the CSV.
- **foodmaker**: only 2 rows (R-021 has no size; R-069 is 455 SF). 1010 Morton St in Mount Vernon (bakery or commissary, with hood) is "Rate Upon Request". The hood-fitted R-045 and R-063 are possible bakery proxies.
- **venue**: no theatre or event-hall lease listing with a rent was found. Church buildings that came up (2201 Greenmount, 1118 N Gilmor) are for sale or off market. Event pages (Peerspace, Co-Balt's $150/hour) price hourly hire, not leases.
- **Waverly storefronts quoted as "contact for pricing"**:
  - 3207 Greenmount (3,150 SF) and 3211 Greenmount (1,740-5,693 SF), both H&R Retail.
  - 2527 Greenmount (1,200 SF office); 1100 E 33rd St (2,675 SF, built 2021).
  - Broker calls would add the most to this tier.
- **HUD metro FMR**: the FY2026 Baltimore-Columbia-Towson MSA FMR could not be read. huduser.gov blocks automated access, and the mirrors show only ZIP-level SAFMR. The SAFMR for 21218 comes from a third-party repost of HUD data (R-014, R-015). FY2027 SAFMR took effect on 2026-10-01 and is lower.
- **ACS**: api.census.gov now needs a key. Figures were taken from the Maryland Department of Planning tract profiles of the 2024 ACS 5-year (R-011 to R-013).
- **Inconsistent or stale listings**:
  - Off market when opened: 5443-5445 Germantown Ave (R-071, R-072).
  - Conflicting asking figures: 4310 Butler St, $12.75 on the listing vs $11.59 in search vs "$3,495+" in the text (R-059); 1214 N Charles St, $24 vs $23 (R-025, R-026); 2125 Maryland Ave office, $1,263 vs "approximately $1700" (R-017).
  - 5607 Germantown Ave: 5,000 SF vs 15,000 SF (R-074).
  - O.C. Haley flyer is dated Dec 2024 (R-075).
- **Distances**: housing distances from Greenmount and E 31st St were not measured. 600 E 37th St (R-009) is at the edge of the half-mile radius.
- **Site access**: LoopNet (.com/.co.uk), Crexi, Craigslist, HUD User and Census Reporter were blocked or did not load listings. LoopNet's French site, CityFeet, CommercialSearch/CommercialCafe and Showcase worked.

### Inferences
- R-001 is the anchor building's own listing (3117 Greenmount, property 742). Use it as the subject's asking rent, not as an independent comp.
- For bike and workshop on Greenmount, the best available stand-ins are small shop rows at $12-$18 NNN, plus warehouse rows ($5-$8) as a floor for back-of-house space.
