# 3200 Greenmount Ave: can it get a liquor licence? (week 5)

**Answer.** Not as a bar. 3200 is in the 43rd alcoholic beverages district, where the Liquor Board may not
issue new licences. The route that fits is the general exception: a Class B licence for a restaurant with at
least $200,000 of capital investment and at least 75 seats, that is, a restaurant that serves drinks. Taking over
an existing licence is the only other route to a bar, and the renewal plan's ban on new taverns would still apply.

This is the team's reading of the statute as students, not legal advice. The Board of Liquor License
Commissioners for Baltimore City is the authority.

## Which district 3200 is in

- Maryland Alcoholic Beverages and Cannabis Article § 12-1603(a): Baltimore City's alcoholic beverages districts
  "at all times are coterminous with the legislative districts in the Legislative Districting Plan of 2022".
- 3200 Greenmount Ave (`properties.csv`, blocklot `0312203882 001`, 39.3274039, -76.6095666) falls in
  **legislative district 43A** on the state's 2022 layer: MD iMAP, `Boundaries/MD_ElectionBoundaries/MapServer/1`
  ("Maryland Legislative Districts 2022"), point query run on 9 November 2026.
- 43A is the Baltimore City part of district 43, so 3200 is in the 43rd alcoholic beverages district.

## What the statute allows there

| Rule | Source | Effect at 3200 |
|---|---|---|
| No new licences in the 40th, 41st, 43rd, 44th and 45th districts | § 12-1603(b) | Applies |
| Class B (beer, wine and liquor) for a restaurant with at least $200,000 capital investment, not counting land and buildings, and at least 75 seats | § 12-1603(c)(1) | The route that fits |
| Block-specific exceptions added by state bills (for example HB 346 of 2025) | § 12-1603(c) | None names Greenmount Ave |
| Transfer of an existing licence | Liquor Board transfer process | Possible in principle; needs a willing holder and Board approval |

- A Class B licence means a real restaurant: the Liquor Board's licensee packet sets a minimum share of food
  sales (41% to 65%, depending on the district) and a working kitchen.
- Class D is the tavern licence. The Waverly urban renewal plan also bars new taverns (sponsor, 26 October;
  the plan text is item 9, pp. 4-29 of BRG48-43-10, flagged in `archive_unfiled_triage_2026-10-26.csv`). The
  exact wording and section are still to be read from those pages.
