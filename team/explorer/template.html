How the candidates are scored

score v0.5 · change the weights and every view updates

After two rule filters, each use is scored on six inputs: heritage, saturation, size, community, neighbours and precedents. Heritage, room, size and neighbours come from the building and its block. Community is the same for every building, and precedents come from a hand-picked library of reuse cases, so neither says much about one building: by default they carry no weight. Change the weights and every view updates. Team and sponsor votes are recorded beside each score; they change it only if you switch that on below. The block plan places uses one at a time under a saturation limit. Rent is kept apart from the score: the comps engine gives the rent level of each space and a sales check for each use. The concepts are written text drafted with AI assistance (Claude) from cited sources, for the team to review.
