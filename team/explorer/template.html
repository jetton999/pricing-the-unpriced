How the recommendation works

score v0.3 · change the weights and every view updates

It is not history alone. After two rule filters, each possible use of a building is scored on six inputs: its heritage, saturation, size, what residents have asked for or objected to, its neighbours, and how similar buildings elsewhere turned out. Team and sponsor votes are recorded beside each score; they change it only if you switch that on below. The block plan places uses one at a time under a saturation limit, and the comps engine below adds what each use could pay and earn. The concepts further down are written text, not computed: drafted with AI assistance (Claude) from each building's records and cited sources, for the team to review, and they say so.
