# Systematic literature review (draft)

Draft systematic literature review for the fall 2026 CUSP deliverable. This is **v2.1 (18 November
2026)**: the 10 November revision with the sponsor's three fixes of 17 November. The October draft
it replaces is in the git history. The final review is due with the Mid-Capstone deliverables on
16 December 2026.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `Literature_Review_Draft.pdf` | The current draft (v2.1) |
| `Literature_Review_Draft.docx` | Editable copy of the same text |

Four review questions: heritage and property value; trade areas, agglomeration, tenant mix and
neighboring uses; vacancy, revitalization and who bears the cost; and the 1940 and 1970s Waverly
programmes as a precedent case. Section 7 covers the methods the project borrows, and Section 8
(Table 2) maps each gap in the literature to what the project has built and tested.

What changed since the October draft:

- 74 sources (46 before), from the sponsor's list, seven OpenAlex searches (two added on
  10 November), citation chasing, and the published research the explorer cites.
- New sections on neighboring uses and street safety (4.4), on who bears the cost when a street
  revives (5.4), and on the methods behind the comps engine, back-test and explorer (7).
- The renewal-plan wording follows the sponsor's clarification of 26 October: new taverns are
  barred, restaurants are not.
- 17 November fixes: design rules on Greenmount come from the renewal plan, not National Register
  listing (3.3); no sentence rests on NDA data (Table 2); the back-test pass rule was agreed on
  9 November (Table 2).

Still to do for 16 December: a second database, counts at each screening step with reasons, a
second person checking a sample of the screening, one table with a row per study, and both the
in-sample and held-out back-test results (including back-test v1).
